1. DiscoverAvistar connects read-only to AWS, Azure and Google Cloud and maps every service account, key, token and AI agent. Grey particles are identities nobody has mapped yet.

  2. PrioritizeEach identity gets an owner and a risk score from low to critical, based on its access and how stale it is. Low risk clears. Critical is pulled aside.

  3. RemediateCritical findings trace back to the pipeline or repo that made them, so the fix lands in your software lifecycle and the identity comes back clean.

  4. RepeatThe loop keeps running, surfacing new, drifting and orphaned credentials before an attacker finds them.
